# Harry / MSG: harmonization with the BSB summer study

Retrospective extension, 2026-09-29 UTC. Earlier Harry Q3 results were already known; this is not preregistration or independent confirmation.

Inputs: the previously audited 39,427 rows from 31 active exports; preserve raw bytes, superseded files and inherited flags. Exclude the two disputed identical exports (2,406 rows). Keep the older Sep 30 export; Oct 2 remains missing. Accepted sample: 37,021 rows, 29 shows. Never count a future, incomplete window as zero activity.

Primary: source sh; 7 < exact days to scheduled start <= 14; numeric ordinary 100 Level Section 101–120 and Row 1–22. Time header EDT is fixed UTC−4; starts 20:00 America/New_York. Boundaries retain <=12 hours, (12,24], (24,48], (48,96], (96,168], >168 hours. Raw clock differences are never rounded before analysis. Per-file cutoff is the most recent 20:00 NY boundary before its final observation, not the current clock date. Source-specific first observation must reach the beginning of each window. Record presence is only a monitoring proxy.

Use the full official 30-date order, including missing Oct 2 at rank 17. Fit log prices on order/10, Section, Row, quantity groups (unknown,1,2,3,4,5plus), exact lead-day bucket and performance weekday. Equal total weight per event. BSB's quantity grouping is used here, replacing Harry Q3's pooled 3–4 category. For price uncertainty take the largest event-cluster, observation-week-cluster and two-way variance; t critical value with min(cluster counts)−1 degrees of freedom. No arbitrary official standard face value is imputed; normalize adjusted price index to the first six events' mean=100, as in BSB.

Activity: count observed eligible records, not tickets or venue attendance. Poisson model with order/10 and weekday; same seven-day exposure for all events. Variance is max(event-week cluster, HC1, overdispersion-scaled information); t(event weeks−1). Source and seat eligibility remain fixed, but actual coverage and inventory denominators are unavailable.

Sensitivity suite: exact Section×Row cells; remove opening; remove observed sample endpoints (NOT the actual residency finale); candidate deduplication; price flag removal; known quantity; front half rows 1–11; other numeric reserved levels (200: Section 201–227 Row 1–25; 400: Section 413–420 Row 1–7); Sep 10 new-markets announcement marker; completed first 15 shows; windows 0–7,14–21,21–28; fixed first-15 cohort for all four windows; start time ±1h. Other-reserved models control level-specific row effects. BSB's staggered announcement-cohort variable is inapplicable to the initially announced Harry 30-date block. The Sep 10 indicator is a confound sensitivity, not a causal event study. No model can separately identify unrestricted calendar trends, lead time and event order in one contiguous run.

The model estimates an average linear ordering association; a negative slope need not mean every later date is cheaper. Intervals are exploratory, not multiple-testing adjusted. Comparing two artists is descriptive, not a controlled experiment or a test that their coefficients differ.

Social: target official HSHQ and Harry Styles separately; Instagram/TikTok/X/Facebook, music/fan communities, contemporary journalism. Record content date, show date, metric date, retrieval time, metric labels, source and limitations. Unlabeled mirror counts and inconsistent dates are excluded from quantitative association. No retrospective current-like-count regression on past ticket prices. Account followers are global, not local demand. Price/supply/primary ticket releases/new 2027 dates and travel budgets are competing explanations for fatigue. News and social availability must be established at prediction time before any trading backtest.

Post-result follow-up, explicitly exploratory: earlier-window activity rose while last-week activity fell for the fixed first 15 shows. To check a timing-shift explanation, add fixed-first15 0–28-day aggregate ordinary100 and all-zone counts, and last-week all-zone counts. These were added after seeing horizon results, not independent confirmatory tests.

Source-driven extension: reading MSG's full Jan 22 announcement confirmed separate general-sale batches (shows 1–10 Jan 30 11 ET; 11–20 Jan 30 14 ET; 21–30 Feb 4 11 ET). They were announced together, but an additional onsale-batch price/count sensitivity is now included. The primary sample spans only the first two same-day batches; this cannot estimate effects of the later Feb 4 batch.
